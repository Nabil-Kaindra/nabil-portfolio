About

I started in software engineering and moved into the kitchen. Now I work between the two.

I'm studying D3 Culinary Arts at Politeknik Bintan Cakrawala, with practical experience in kitchen operations, food quality checking and food and beverage service. I'm building my pastry and baking fundamentals and want to learn from experienced pastry chefs.

Before that, I studied Software Engineering at SMKN 4 Tanjungpinang Timur. I still design in Canva, write front-end code and manage websites, and I led the Kominfo (communication and information) team at BEM PBC. I bring the same habits to both worlds: clean workflow, attention to detail, and respect for the team around me.
